Questions

Before you buy

Does Edgebook upload my trades anywhere?

No. Your journal, charts and notes are stored on your computer. Edgebook only goes online to check for updates, to activate your license key, to fetch a TradingView snapshot when you paste its link, and to download its PDF tool the first time you make a monthly report.

Which brokers can I import from?

Edgebook reads CSV trade history from Interactive Brokers, Schwab / thinkorswim, Tradovate, MetaTrader 4 and 5, and Binance. For any other broker, you match the columns once. You can also log trades by hand in a few seconds.

Which asset classes does it handle?

Stocks, options, futures, forex, crypto, CFDs and ETFs, including contract multipliers, option strikes and expiries, fees and swap, across several accounts and currencies.

Does it work on a Mac?

Edgebook runs on Windows 10 and 11 today. A Mac version is on the way, and your license will cover it.

I already use the earlier Edgebook. Will I lose my journal?

No. The first time the new app opens, it offers to move your journal across in one click. The old copy stays where it is.

Do I need Vault?

No. Vault is an optional extra for automatic backup, sync and broker import. The $5 app is the complete journal.

Is Edgebook financial advice?

No. Edgebook measures your own trades so you can review them. It does not recommend trades or give investment advice.
