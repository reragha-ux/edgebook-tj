Bring your journal into the new Edgebook

We found a journal from the earlier Edgebook on this PC. Move it here so you can keep working with your trades, charts and notes. The old copy stays where it is.

If the old Edgebook is open, it closes first and saves anything you were typing.

You can also move it by hand:

  1. Open the old Edgebook and go to Settings → Data → Backup.
  2. Here, click Start fresh, then go to Settings → Data → Restore and pick that file.
